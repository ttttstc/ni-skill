# Gathered Scenes Zine v1.3

Use only when the Style Router selects **Gathered Scenes**. This is a compact port of `Zeejay0/gathered-scenes-zine-skill/skills/scenes-gathered-zine-v1-3`. Keep its photo-truthful behavior separate from Scene Distillation.

## Core identity

Build `真景为锚、插画成场、色彩成结构、撕纸成界、纸面会呼吸`:

- keep the supplied photographic scene truthful and recognizable;
- enlarge selected source forms into a calm abstract illustration field instead of tracing the photo;
- compress foliage, branches, crowds, texture, and repeated detail into a few large graphic masses;
- derive one added hue from a source shape and make it change balance, movement, figure–ground, or meaning;
- use a visibly hand-torn fibrous edge at the main photo-to-paper handoff;
- make omission and negative space active parts of the composition.

Priority: scene identity and key spatial relationship, truthful photo, simplification, large designed field, shared source skeleton, structural hue, quiet space, torn edge, then one restrained micro-text line.

## Scene Card

Inspect the supplied photo first. Resolve:

- **Core subjects:** one or two elements that identify the scene.
- **Supporting elements:** two or three place or atmosphere cues.
- **Spatial invariants:** horizon, relative position, scale, perspective, direction, overlap, path, or silhouette that must survive.
- **Dominant gesture:** strongest horizontal, vertical, diagonal, curve, convergence, gaze, or movement.
- **Visual-weight map:** area, darkness, saturation, texture, faces, isolation, and edge tension.
- **Native color atmosphere:** hue family, temperature, value range, and existing saturated areas.
- **Source-shape candidates:** one or two silhouettes, planes, shadows, paths, architectural rhythms, or atmospheric forms that can continue through photo, illustration, and color.
- **Natural quiet areas:** sky, water, wall, ground, haze, or other low-information regions.
- **Semantic minimum:** smallest set of forms and relationships that still identifies this particular scene.

Treat the photo as factual source and the illustration as interpretation of that fact.

## Abstraction and illustration

Use medium abstraction by default: keep the core subject, dominant gesture, and one key relationship; remove roughly 60–80% of small descriptive detail; merge repeated forms; replace realistic shading with flat ink, broken contour, sparse hatching, halftone, or cut paper; leave deliberate paper gaps.

Choose one primary illustration grammar and at most one supporting grammar:

- **Silhouette-led:** broad mass for figures, trees, roofs, boats, or profiles.
- **Contour-led:** broken lines for architecture, paths, railings, coastlines, or gestures.
- **Field-led:** irregular ink/halftone field for water, fog, sky, shadow, or ground.
- **Rhythm-led:** sparse repeated marks for windows, waves, branches, steps, or crowds.
- **Cut-paper-led:** one or two simplified organic/geometric shapes when the source has a clear shape hierarchy.

For dense foliage or organic detail, retain one main canopy/tree mass, one to three directional branch gestures, at most two secondary clusters, and one sparse texture sample. Omit roughly 85–95% of individual leaves, needles, fine twigs, and repeated marks. If the field reads as lace, filigree, or a botanical engraving at thumbnail size, merge forms and remove marks.

Keep the illustration field large but quiet: field extent roughly 45–70% of the poster, 55–75% of that field visibly unprinted; for intricate scenes, 65–85% of the field quiet and active illustration only about 10–25% of the whole poster. Use one dominant mass, one or two supporting marks, one restrained texture field, and no more than two neutral ink values besides paper and the added hue.

## Composition and torn handoff

Choose from source geometry rather than habit:

- **Transformative seam:** photo about 30–50%, one real subject/horizon/path/shadow continues as illustration.
- **Underprint overlay:** photo about 35–60%, broad low-density illustration and hue pass behind/through it without damaging defining detail.
- **Photo anchor + expansive field:** photo about 25–45%, illustration field about 45–70%.
- **Directional split:** align a 55/45 to 65/35 split with a real horizon, gaze, path, edge, or convergence.
- **Irregular fragments:** combined photo fragments about 25–50%, joined by one shared illustration mass or color structure.

The main photo-to-paper or photo-to-illustration boundary is a real paper object:

- irregular hand-ripped contour with shallow notches, uneven rises, soft scallops, and one or two longer fiber pulls;
- narrow warm paper/fiber fringe, slight abrasion or pigment loss near selected segments;
- flat scan behavior, no lifted-paper depth;
- active fiber band roughly 1–4% of the poster short edge and visible across roughly 35–70% of the photographic perimeter;
- illustration or hue may cross selected torn segments, while the tear remains legible;
- speckles/halftone crumbs/ghost marks are subordinate and limited to one or two pressure points.

Avoid clean clipping paths, crisp rectangular masks, uniform white outlines, sticker borders, decorative deckled frames, heavy shadows, curled corners, layered-paper depth, or equal torn effects on every edge.

## Structural color

Use one added print hue, chosen by source relation: quiet analogous harmony, focused complement, temperature bridge, or meaningful source-color resonance. Specify an exact hue and paper-native material such as saturated cobalt risograph ink, opaque ultramarine cut paper, clean tomato-red block, vivid pear-green silhouette, lemon-yellow dry print, or saturated magenta-pink mass.

Choose one integration mode:

- **Source continuation:** extend a roofline, road, cloud, window, garment, shadow, plant, vehicle panel, or other source-specific form beyond the photo edge.
- **Selective replacement:** convert one photographic region into a flat saturated shape while preserving surrounding scene.
- **Underprint passage:** broad translucent/halftone/misregistered field passes behind and through photo and illustration.
- **Counterform:** derive a colored positive/negative shape from a silhouette, gap, shadow, or quiet area.
- **Directional rhythm:** stretch/repeat one source-derived form along a gaze, path, railing, ridge, shoreline, or convergence.

Make the color pass at least two tests: its contour/position/rhythm derives from the scene; it touches, overlaps, replaces, passes behind/through the photo or illustration; it crosses the photo-paper boundary; it redirects the eye; or it reveals/intensifies a real relationship. If removing the hue changes nothing, redesign it. Typical area: 2–6% for opaque replacement/cut paper, 6–15% for translucent/halftone underprint, 10–20% for a large structural field.

## Micro-text

Add only one quiet editorial line unless the user explicitly asks otherwise. Use supplied wording verbatim. Otherwise author one compact scene-related line. English-only is default; Chinese-only or Chinese–English pairing only when requested. Keep Chinese to 8 Han characters or fewer, English to 5 words or fewer. No dates, coordinates, labels, archive stamps, or pseudo-metadata by default.

Use a small typewriter serif/monospace, faint handwriting, pencil, worn stamp, or dry ink; keep it 1.3–3.5% of poster height, subordinate to photo and illustration, placed in quiet paper near an edge/axis/baseline. Preserve exact spelling and integrate letters into the same flat scan texture.

## Prompt and workflow

Compile four compact paragraphs:

1. canvas, attention geometry, photo/illustration shares, focal area, quiet field, eye path, text area;
2. scene invariants and what remains photographic;
3. illustration grammar and abstraction map; structural hue and material; torn contour/fibers; exact micro-text and placement;
4. paper/scan mood, legibility, and hard avoids.

Workflow: inspect photo, build Scene Card, choose eye path and source shapes, choose composition and abstraction map, simplify organic detail, choose one grammar, design torn handoff, choose hue/integration/function, resolve micro-text, compile, generate with the supplied photo, inspect at normal and thumbnail scale, regenerate at most once for one observed failure.

## Output contract

By default return the generated image and one brief Chinese rationale explaining the source-derived composition, enlarged illustration field, structural hue, and emotional intention. Reveal the full prompt or detailed composition notes only when the user explicitly asks.

If generation fails, return the concrete failure and the compiled prompt only if the user asked for the prompt; do not pretend that a photo was preserved.

## Hard avoids

No literal tracing, leaf-by-leaf rendering, dense botanical filigree, full-scene photocopy, timid peripheral doodle, generic abstract motifs, detached color swatch, arbitrary bright dot, clean photo mask, sticker border, heavy paper shadow, dense scrapbook, uniform dotted border, multiple illustration styles, multiple added hues, commercial hierarchy, logo/CTA, glossy mockup, neon, 3D, cinematic lighting, depth of field, fashion drama, cute cartoon/anime, large polished typography, long text, invented quotation, faux metadata, or watermark.
