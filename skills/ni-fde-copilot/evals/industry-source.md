# Source

Board memo for a B2B SaaS company.

The company reports 118 percent net revenue retention and 91 percent gross revenue retention. Management attributes growth to a land-and-expand motion. A cohort chart shows the January cohort falling from 100 customers to 88 after one year while revenue from the remaining customers rises from 10 million to 11.8 million yuan. The memo calls this proof that churn is solved.

Customer Success is compensated on renewals, Sales on new annual contract value, and Product on weekly active accounts. Enterprise customers can reduce seats at renewal without cancelling. The memo does not define whether expansion from acquired companies belongs to the cohort, and Finance uses a different exchange-rate convention from Sales.
